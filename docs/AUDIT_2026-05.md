## Open decisions (documented choices)

### Blog vs review canonical

**Decision:** Both URL families stay indexed. `/blog/[slug]/` is the long-form editorial surface (BlogPosting schema). `/review/[product-slug]/` is the catalogue-integrated product page (finder-linked, fit-score context).

**Rationale:** Blog URLs carry historical SEO equity and narrative depth; review pages serve commercial intent and product schema. Neither is `noindex`'d nor consolidated to a single canonical.

**Implementation:** `src/lib/content-links.ts` links blog ↔ review via `blog-review-product-map.json`. `blogSlugForReview()` ranks multiple blogs per product by slug keywords and section depth. Each page emits its own canonical (`/blog/…/` or `/review/…/`).

### E-E-A-T blocks

**Decision:** Lightweight optional fields in JSON only — no mandatory sitewide E-E-A-T component in May 2026.

**Rationale:** Author byline (`Rui Su`), `/authors/rui-su/`, methodology page, and claims registry already cover core signals. Full `ReviewMethodologyBox` UI is queued in Q2 plan (§3.2 #13).

**Current state:** Legacy TS articles may include `methodology` story blocks; Option B JSON articles use `verdict` + first-person sections. Future optional JSON keys (`testingNotes`, `authorCredentials`) can be added without layout churn.

### Duplicate URL strategy

**Decision:** Both indexed with cross-links; duplicate pairs differentiated with disambiguation intros — not redirects or `noindex`.

**Rationale:** Preserves slug continuity (e.g. Anders Antonsen correction URL), captures distinct search intents (standalone vs comparison vs deep-dive), avoids equity loss from mass redirects.

**Implementation:** `SLUG_DISAMBIGUATION` in `scripts/blog-import-option-b.py` prepends a one-sentence scope note to the overview when bodies would otherwise match. Pairs include AxForce 90 New 5U vs broader comparison, Astrox 100ZZ correction slug, gen-1 vs gen-2 Astrox 99 Pro, dual HS Plus articles, RSL shuttle variants, FZ Forza 88D variants, Bladex 800 vs Halbertec 9000 Power.
