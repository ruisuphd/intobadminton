# SEO, ROI, and review-reference strategy

## Principle

The highest-ROI path is a trusted recommendation engine, not a copied-review index.

## SEO clusters

- Beginner racket advice.
- Doubles / mixed speed rackets.
- Smash-heavy singles rackets.
- Racket family comparisons (Astrox vs Nanoflare, Victor vs Yonex, Li-Ning vs Yonex).
- Shoe fit pages (wide feet, ankle/knee comfort).
- String tension by level and style.

Each page should include original analysis, methodology links, a quiz CTA, updated date, and source policy.

## AdSense ROI

- Keep quiz/results low-ad and high-trust.
- Put ads on guide/comparison pages after the introduction and between major sections.
- Segment AdSense channels by page type: guides, comparisons, results, quiz.
- Optimize RPM only after measuring quiz completion, return visits, and Core Web Vitals.

## Review references

### BadmintonCN

Use search-visible metadata and source links as reference signals:

- source name;
- original language;
- high-level theme summary;
- confidence;
- link.

Do not show copied Chinese excerpts unless permission allows it.

### Reddit

Treat as permission-gated. Do not scrape or commercialize Reddit data without approval or a compliant provider.

### First-party reviews

Prioritize consented IntoBadminton reviews. They are the long-term moat and safest basis for recommendation quality.
