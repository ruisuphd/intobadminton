## chengzhen testing protocol (so we know how to weight his findings)

- Weighs unstrung, then strung with underbase removed, then balance point.
- Uses N65, 80, AB, ABBT, Z61 strings at 26-28 lb typically.
- Tests with multiple players from his regular men's doubles group across skill levels.
- References Yuan-style (源式) shaft hardness measurements where available
  (lower number = stiffer): 100ZZ ~6.71, 99 Pro 2 ~7.43, 88DP New Color 7.59,
  Halbertec 9000 Power 7.65, Arcsaber 11 Pro 7.86, Halbertec 9000 7.92,
  Halbertec 8000 8.33, Bladex 800 Speed 7.83, Bladex 800 New 8.58.
- Owns 5x 100XSE, 2x 800 Pro, 2x 1000Z, plus NF700 Pro, 黑金隼 SE, 极音速 (Hayabusa)
  and Bladex 900 New — heavy speed-racket bias in his rotation.

## Product-by-product findings (cited in `products.json`)

### Yonex Astrox 88D Pro (2024 new color)
- 4U/G6 sample 84g unstrung; G5 sample also ~84g (10+ samples averaged).
- Strung weight 89.5–91.1g depending on string + underbase; balance 305–308mm.
- Frame narrower than original camel-gold; stronger pocketing, higher net pressure.
- Shaft uses 2nd-gen Namd → faster snapback than camel-gold by ~1 level.
- Smash power similar between gens; new color has better continuity, lower fatigue.
- chengzhen ranks it above 100ZZ and original 88DP for overall package.
- Slightly stiffer shaft than Lei Ting 90 New; weaker on net-play pocketing.

### Yonex Astrox 88S Pro (2024 new color)
- 4U sample 84.3g unstrung, 89.5g strung w/ 80@26-28lb, balance 301mm.
- Frame one size bigger than 88DP New; slightly shorter handle and overall length.
- Shaft hardness similar to NF800 Pro; slightly stiffer than 77 Pro.
- chengzhen calls it his #1 mixed doubles main racket.
- "Peak of control-balance frames now"; better than Halberds 8000/9000/9000P + Arcsaber 11 Pro for combined control + smash.
- Pocketing makes it ideal for mixed doubles (every shot must be solid).
- Same shaft hardness range as 9000P (~7.5-7.6) but very different feel.

### Yonex Astrox 100ZZ (regular)
- chengzhen acknowledges iconic status but says new-color 88DP outperforms it overall.
- 100ZZ success partly attributed to Yonex brand strength + national-team endorsement.
- Many amateur buyers struggle to drive it.

### Yonex Astrox 100 ZZ VA
- Not directly reviewed by chengzhen, but he references the Nanoflare 100XSE
  as the standard speed-racket benchmark; if 100ZZ feels punishing, 100XSE
  sits a tier easier.

### Yonex Nanoflare 700 Pro (2024)
- 4U/G5: 83.6g unstrung, 88.7g w/ underbase removed, balance 299mm.
- 5U/G5: 79.6g unstrung, 84.6g w/ underbase removed, balance 305mm.
- 3 samples of 4U weighed 83.6/84.8/85.8g — meaningful variance.
- Lighter sample with lower balance still smashed better than heavier NF700 →
  shaft hardness > gram-level mass for attack quality.
- Shaft only marginally stiffer than NF700; new SF filter + enhanced Sonic
  Flare add clarity, elasticity, easier high clears, easier defense.
- Lower bend point than NF700 → easier downward pressure on smashes.

### Yonex Nanoflare 700 (Game)
- Most popular "sugar-water" (糖水) speed racket per chengzhen.
- Broad audience: from beginners to higher-level players who want relaxed front-court.
- chengzhen's wife switched to it from NS9000s and stuck.
- Soft-medium shaft, head-light; less control precision than 700 Pro.
- Bend point closer to handle than 700 Pro → harder downward pressure.

### Yonex Nanoflare 800 Pro (2024) — added in this PR
- 4U/G5: 85.2g unstrung, 89.7g w/ underbase removed, balance 301mm.
- Crisp/snap (脆弹) feel, metallic ringing tone.
- Swing speed faster than 100XSE and 1000Z.
- Frame anti-torsion excellent, fast snapback, 78-hole stringbed (vs usual 76),
  copper foil at frame base.
- Best in series for drives + flat exchanges; ideal for fast men's doubles.
- Net play: easier to reach high contact point but harder to control drop placement.
- Rear court: weaker than 1000Z (no DR carbon → less pocketing).
- Higher entry threshold than NF700 Pro; sharper smash placement than non-Pro 100X.

### Yonex Nanoflare 1000Z
- 4U/G5: 84g unstrung, 89.3g w/ underbase removed, balance 304mm.
- chengzhen's "hexagonal warrior" speed racket — most balanced of all speed
  rackets, no clear weakness.
- DR carbon gives pocketing feel unusual for a small-frame fast racket.
- Best end-speed + pointing accuracy in Nanoflare line.
- Higher entry threshold than NF700 Pro (shaft ~2 levels stiffer).
- Together with 100XSE, ranked T0-tier speed racket.

## Brand naming reference (these matter — earlier draft had errors)

Chinese-language reviews use brand-internal series names that do not always
map cleanly to English. Confirmed mappings:

- **战戟 (Zhan Ji "war halberd")** = **Li-Ning Halbertec** series. NOT Victor.
- **锋影 (Feng Ying "sharp shadow")** = **Li-Ning Bladex** series.
- **雷霆 (Lei Ting "thunder")** = Li-Ning attack series (80, 90 龙虎,
  90 New, 100). Distinct from AxForce.
- **AxForce** = Li-Ning AxForce (90, 99) — different again from Lei Ting.
- **神速 (Shen Su "speed god")** = Victor Hayabusa series.
- **极音速 (Ji Yin Su "extreme sonic speed")** = Victor Auraspeed Hayabusa.
- **黑金隼 (Hei Jin Sun "black gold falcon")** = Victor Hayabusa SE / Black
  Gold colorway.
- **突击 / 突击者 (Tu Ji "thrust/raid")** = Victor Thruster K series.
- **疾光 (Ji Guang "fast light")** = Yonex Nanoflare line.

When in doubt: **战戟 + 锋影 + 雷霆 + AxForce = Li-Ning. 神速 + 极音速 +
黑金隼 + 突击 = Victor.**

### Li-Ning — Phase C-4
- **Halbertec 8000 (战戟 8000)** — chengzhen's #1 amateur recommendation. Cost-
  effective vs Astrox 88S Pro New Color (~$400-500 cheaper). Best balance-
  racket smash with underbase removed. Box-frame.
- **Halbertec 9000 (战戟 9000)** — 4U/G5 reviewed; thinner frame than 8000;
  faster but weaker rear-court solidity. "卸力" (loses power) on hard smash.
  Marketed as "control king" but reviewer disagrees with that positioning.
- **Halbertec 9000 Power (战戟 9000P)** — 4U/G5: 83.5g unstrung, 86.4g w/
  underbase removed, balance 307mm. Yuan-style hardness 7.65. Sample variance:
  5/10 samples ~82.5g (very light). Speed-racket profile, NOT a Halbertec 8000
  successor in feel.
- **Bladex 800 Speed (锋影 800 Speed)** — 4U/G6: 85.2g unstrung, 90.8g
  grip+string, balance 299mm. Yuan-style hardness 7.83 (very stiff for
  speed racket). Frame torsion 18.72 (best in speed-racket category, even
  better than 99 Pro 2 at 19.87). M46X carbon → "tough-elastic" (韧弹) feel
  with longer dwell vs other speed rackets.
- **Bladex 800 New (锋影 800 New)** — 4U/G5: 84g unstrung, 89.3g w/ underbase
  removed, balance 297mm. Yuan-style hardness 8.58 (softest among high-end
  speed rackets). chengzhen rated only 3 stars.
- **Lei Ting 90 New (雷霆 90 New)** — 4U: 89.5g w/ underbase removed, balance
  304mm. 6.4mm shaft (vs 6.2mm in 90 龙虎 predecessor). chengzhen calls it
  Li-Ning's best shaft yet, replaces Lei Ting 80 in his rotation. Small frame
  but forgiving sweet spot.
- **Lei Ting 80 (雷霆 80)** — 4U: 89.2g w/ underbase removed, balance 304mm.
  Sweeter "sugar-water" entry-friendly attack racket; chengzhen plans to retire it.
