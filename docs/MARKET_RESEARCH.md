# Market Research Notes

Last reviewed: 2026-04-28

## Source Use Policy

- BadmintonCN, Reddit, and YouTube are treated as rights-limited evidence sources.
- The app stores source links, themes, confidence, and short human summaries.
- The app does not copy full review text, translate forum posts, republish user photos, or use transcripts without rights review.
- Official brand pages remain the source of truth for specifications.

## Findings Implemented

1. BadmintonCN pages often expose reviewer profile context such as level, body size, ownership age, price, and tags. The app now treats those as evidence signals rather than direct facts.
2. Reddit string discussions repeatedly split control strings like BG80 from repulsive strings like EXBOLT 63. The app now scores strings as their own category with gauge, feel, repulsion, control, durability, and tension range.
3. YouTube racket reviews are useful for role explanations, especially doubles role splits like rear-court power vs front-court speed. They are linked as review evidence, not official specs.
4. Shoe recommendations need foot-width, cushioning, and stability inputs because return/injury risk is higher than for rackets. The app now includes shoe scoring.
5. Bags are workflow products. Capacity, shoe compartments, and wet storage create repeat-use content and pre-session checklists. The app now includes bag scoring.
6. Resale value changes the real cost of ownership. Rackets can retain value when authentic and popular; strings are consumables; shoes and bags depreciate heavily with hygiene/condition.

## Commercial UX Direction

- Encourage repeat visits through gear refresh guides, depreciation checks, string-tension logs, and session loadout articles.
- Keep recommendations evidence-aware so community hype cannot override official specs or safety/comfort cautions.
- Add affiliate links only after source verification, paid-placement disclosure, and a clear separation from the fit score.
